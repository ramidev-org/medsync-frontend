🏥 Closed Medical Clinic Management System

(Internal staff only – no patient login)

👥 USER ROLES (Internal)

1️⃣ Admin
2️⃣ Doctor (General / Specialist)
3️⃣ Assistant / Reception / Nurse

🔐 AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL
Tables

users

roles

permissions

user_roles

role_permissions

audit_logs

Features

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Secure login / logout

Password reset

Account activation / suspension

Full audit trail (who did what, when)

👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR MODULE

(Medical + Consultation – Read-only access to appointments)

A. Doctor Profile

View / edit own profile

Specialty management

Consultation pricing

Availability schedule (used by assistant)

Digital signature

B. Appointment Viewing & Schedule

View daily / weekly / monthly agenda

View appointment details

View patient basic info

Update appointment status only:

Pending

In consultation

Completed

Cancelled

No-show

View emergency walk-ins assigned by assistant
❌ Cannot create, reschedule, or assign appointments

C. Consultation Management

Open consultation session

Add consultation notes

Diagnosis (ICD codes)

Medical observations

Treatment plan

Follow-up recommendations

Close consultation

D. Prescriptions

Create prescriptions

Drug dosage & duration

Reusable prescription templates

Digital signing

Print / PDF export

E. Medical Records (Internal)

(Patients exist but never log in)

View full patient history

Previous consultations

Previous prescriptions

Uploaded documents (PDF, scans)

Attach lab results & imaging reports

F. Orders & Medical Requests

Lab test requests

Imaging requests (X-ray, MRI, CT)

Referral letters (internal / external)

Sick leave certificates

Medical certificates

G. Doctor Statistics (View Only)

Number of consultations

Revenue per period

Common diagnoses

Consultation duration statistics

🔄 WORKFLOW EXAMPLE

1️⃣ Assistant creates appointment
2️⃣ Patient arrives → assistant checks in
3️⃣ Doctor views agenda & opens consultation
4️⃣ Doctor adds diagnosis & prescription
5️⃣ Assistant processes payment
6️⃣ Admin reviews reports & audit logs
